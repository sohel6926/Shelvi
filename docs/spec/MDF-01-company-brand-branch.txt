 3. COMPANY RULES - FROZEN
 1. Every Brand belongs to exactly one Company.

 2. Every Branch ultimately belongs to one Company.

 3. Financial transactions must know their Company.

 4. Invoice numbering cannot cross legal entities unless explicitly configured.

 5. GST/tax settings belong primarily to the legal entity or tax registration.

 6. Company deletion is forbidden after business transactions exist.

 7. Company changes must be audited.

 8. Sensitive company tax/bank changes require authorized approval.

 5. BRAND RULES - FROZEN
 1. Brand must always belong to a Company.

 2. Brand cannot own accounting independently unless mapped to the proper legal entity.

 3. Brand revenue and cost reports must be separately available.

 4. Customer can have bookings across multiple Brands.

 5. The same Vendor can supply multiple Brands.

 6. Items may be shared or Brand-restricted.

 7. Recipes may be global or Brand-specific.

 8. Brand-specific selling prices are allowed.

 9. Brand-specific logos/documents must not retroactively change finalized historical invoices.

 10. Archived Brand remains visible in historical records.

 7. BRANCH RULES - FROZEN
 1. Every Event belongs to an operating Branch.

 2. Every employee has a home Branch unless centrally assigned.

 3. Inventory stock always belongs to a physical stock location under a Branch.

 4. Customer itself need not permanently belong to one Branch.

 5. Booking is assigned to a Branch.

 6. Event may be transferred to another Branch only through an approved transfer.

 7. Branch transfer must retain history.

 8. Branch-specific price lists are allowed.

 9. Branch-specific approval limits are allowed.

 10. Branch reporting must include Revenue, Cost, Stock, Staff and Profit.

 9. KITCHEN RULES - FROZEN
 1. Production Batch must belong to a Kitchen.

 2. One Event Session can receive products from multiple Kitchens.

 3. Kitchen is not equal to Store.

 4. Raw material must move into Kitchen through Stock Movement.

 5. Production output must create proper production inventory/consumption records where applicable.

 6. Kitchen transfer must be auditable.

 7. Veg/Non-Veg restrictions cannot be bypassed without authorized configuration.

 8. Kitchen closure/inactivation cannot remove historical production data.

 13. STOCK LOCATION SUB-STRUCTURE
 Optional detailed hierarchy:
 Store -> Zone -> Rack -> Shelf -> Bin

 Example: Main Grocery Store -> Zone A -> Rack 03 -> Shelf 02 -> Bin 05

 This is optional for small operations, but the architecture must support it.


 14. STORE RULES - FROZEN
 1. Stock cannot exist without a Stock Location.

 2. Store balance cannot be directly typed or edited.

 3. Every increase/decrease comes from a Stock Movement.

 4. Transfer between Stores creates an Out movement and an In movement.

 5. Event Venue stock is treated as a valid temporary location.

 6. Vehicle/Transit can be a temporary inventory location.

 7. Quarantine stock cannot be issued normally.

 8. Expired stock cannot be issued normally.

 9. Scrap/Waste stock cannot return to usable inventory without an approved reversal.

 10. Physical count variance requires an adjustment transaction and approval.

 23. MAIN RELATIONSHIP RULE
 Company: 1 -> Many Brands

 Brand: Many -> Many Branches where shared branches are required

 Branch: 1 -> Many Kitchens

 Branch: 1 -> Many Stores

 Kitchen: 1 -> Many Sections

 Store: 1 -> Many Stock Locations


 24. IMPORTANT DESIGN DECISION
 Brand and Branch should not be hard-coded as one-to-one.

 Example: one Hyderabad Central Kitchen may serve multiple approved Brands. Therefore architecture must allow
 one operational Branch/Kitchen to serve multiple Brands while every transaction still records the responsible
 Brand/Event.


 25. TRANSACTION LINKING RULE
 Purchase
 Company + Branch + Store + Event/Overhead + Brand where applicable.

 Production
 Company + Branch + Kitchen + Event + Session + Product.

 Sale
 Company + Brand + Branch + Customer + Booking + Event.
