2. Golden rules (apply to every screen)
1. One number, one source. Every money/target/guest figure is calculated once on the server
   (Postgres function or Edge Function) and every screen reads that result. No money maths in React
   components.
2. Auto / Manual on every calculated value. Auto = CRM calculates; Manual = a user types it.
   Show which one is used and who set it, e.g. "Manual · Sainadh · 10 Nov".
3. Actual vs estimate. Numbers from real bills are shown plainly. Estimates are labelled "Estimate"
   in a different colour. Never show a made-up number as real.
4. No placeholder data in production. Hard-coded demo values (fixed biryani plan, fixed
   suggested price, fixed P&L series) must be removed; show "No data yet — add X" instead.
5. Audit log for every change to money, rates, targets, roles: who, when, old value, new value.
6. Three languages: Telugu, English, Hindi — UI and customer messages (see §7).
7. Refresh button on every page plus "Refreshed x min ago".

3. Calculations (exact rules)

3.1 Targets      MUST

 Month target = Year target × month season weight ÷ sum of 12 weights (largest-remainder
 split; parts add up exactly)

 Needed per day = max(0, Month target − Month booked so far) ÷ days left including today

   Year target is set per business on the Year target page and stored in one column. Suseela
   Caterings: ₹1,50,00,000, profit 25%.
   "All businesses" view = sum of each business's own target, never a default. No hidden defaults (₹1
   crore, ₹2.5 crore).
   Dashboard, Target board, Year target and Reports must all show the same target for the same
   business and month.
   Example: ₹1.5 crore with equal weights → ₹12,50,000 per month. Booked ₹9,70,000 on 10 Nov with
   21 days left → ₹13,334 per day.

3.3 Plate cost and profit          MUST

 Dish cost per 100 guests = Σ (ingredient qty per 100 × ingredient rate)

 Plate cost = Σ dish cost ÷ 100 + gas + disposables + (event wages ÷ guests) + (transport ÷
 guests) + waste %

 Margin % = (Plate price − Plate cost) ÷ Plate price × 100

 Minimum price for target margin = Plate cost ÷ (1 − target margin)

   Ingredient rate: Auto = average of last 3 purchase bills; Manual allowed.
   For "/" choice groups use the chosen option; before the choice, use the costliest and say so.
   Colours on the quote: ≥30% green, 25–30% amber, <25% red (red needs owner approval). This is
   event (gross) margin; yearly net profit also subtracts rent, salaries and ads.
   Show fixed costs (transport, minimum staff) separately so small events are not misjudged.
   When a new purchase rate is saved: re-cost all dishes and menus that use it; list menus/quotes
   whose margin fell below target.
Example: veg wedding menu, 500 guests, ingredients ₹90 + gas ₹4 + disposables ₹8 + wages ₹40 + transport ₹10
+ waste ₹4.50 = ₹156.50; at ₹250 margin = 37.4%; minimum price at 25% = ₹208.67.


3.4 Waste and learning           SHOULD

 Eaten per 100 = (cooked kg − leftover kg) ÷ guests × 100 ; next plan per 100 (Auto) =
 average of last 5 events × 1.05

Leftovers entered by one tap per dish: None / Little / Half / Lot (converted to an estimated kg, labelled
estimate). Alert when waste > 8%.

3.5 Stock     SHOULD

 Expected = opening + purchased − planned use ; Variance = expected − counted ; alert if
 variance > 3% of use

 Re-order when stock ≤ average daily use × (vendor lead days + 2)

Physical count monthly for the 15 costliest items only. Call it "variance", not theft.

3.6 Hotel / hostel supply and meal cards                MUST

   Contract per customer: meal types and rate, weekly menu, minimum plates, billing day, credit
   days.
   Daily entry by exception: "All delivered ✓ except …". Warden/hotel confirms the count by WhatsApp
   yes/no.
   Monthly bill = Σ (accepted plates × rate) + extras − advance. Customer profit = bill − Σ (plates ×
   that day's plate cost) − transport.
   Meal card / subscription: prepaid balance, pause / skip / holiday, part-month billing, low-balance
   alert.
   Receivable ageing per customer: 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days.

3.7 Payment reminders            MUST

Events: balance due 2 days before the event; reminders 7 days before, 2 days before, the day after,
then every 7 days. Hotel/hostel: due = bill date + credit days; reminders on due day, +3, +7. Stop
automatically when paid. Per-customer pause switch.

5. WhatsApp Business API (our own number)
   Use both normal WhatsApp and the API on the same number ("Coexistence"): staff keep chatting
   from the WhatsApp Business app on the phone, while the CRM sends automatic messages through
   the Cloud API, with chats synced. The number must be registered on the WhatsApp Business app
   (not personal WhatsApp) and connected through a provider that supports Coexistence onboarding.
   Planned volume about 5,000 messages a day.
   One WhatsApp number per brand (Suseela Caterings, Rambabu Kitchen, Daiva Sankalp, and
   any later brand). Settings → WhatsApp: add/remove numbers and link each to a brand. Every
   message goes out from the number of the brand that owns the booking, quote or invoice; replies
   come back to that brand's inbox; templates, name and logo are per brand. Each number connects
   separately (Coexistence per number).
   No misuse between brands: each brand's number, inbox, customers' chats and templates are
   separate; staff see only the brands assigned to them; only the owner can add, change or remove a
   number; WhatsApp access tokens are stored only on the server (never in the app code — see Code
   Review C-1); every message is logged with who sent it; bulk sends need owner approval and go
   only to customers who agreed to receive messages, so the number is not blocked.
   Message types as approved templates: enquiry reply, quote with approve link, booking
   confirmation, payment receipt, payment reminder, invoice PDF, event-day details, feedback
   request, delivery/headcount confirmation (yes/no), vendor purchase order.
   Send in the customer's language (Telugu / English / Hindi). Use readable event names, never codes
   (e.g. "Traditional ceremony", not traditional_ceremony ).
   Incoming replies (yes/no, ratings, "pause tomorrow") update the CRM automatically.
  Log every message (sent / delivered / read / failed) on the customer and booking.
  Until API approval: show a "Copy & send" button (wa.me link) so nothing is blocked.
  Respect opt-out; store consent.

13.9 Customer data and privacy                  MUST

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 (notified November 2025;
most duties apply from May 2027) cover customer names and phone numbers. Build it in now:

   Collect only what is needed. Show a short notice in the customer's language wherever data is taken
   (website form, QR, card).
   Separate consent for service messages and for offers. Stopping must be as easy as agreeing
   ("STOP").
   A customer can ask to see, correct or delete their data. Bills are kept as long as tax law requires;
   other data is deleted or anonymised after a set period (setting).
  Only the owner can export the customer list; every export is logged. Kitchen staff see masked
  phone numbers.
  If data leaks: alert the owner at once and follow the Rules' reporting steps (Data Protection Board
  and affected customers, within 72 hours).
  Confirm the final wording with our CA / lawyer.


13.10 Getting paid: payment links, advance, cancellation, refund                        MUST

  Every quote, invoice and reminder carries a UPI QR and UPI pay link with the amount filled in.
  With a payment gateway (optional switch) the receipt is recorded automatically; for direct UPI the
  staff enters the UTR number (duplicates refused, T18).
  Advance rule per brand (default 30%, §3.2); balance due 2 days before the event (§3.7).
  Cancellation policy per brand as a setting, printed on the quote and the confirmation. Example:
  more than 15 days before → advance refunded minus 10%; 7–15 days → 50% of advance kept; less
  than 7 days → advance kept. Cost already spent is always recorded.
  Refund = credit note plus refund receipt, owner approval needed.
  Driver's cash: collected → handed to accounts → matched; mismatch alerts the owner.
