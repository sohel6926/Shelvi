 GLOBAL TRANSACTION RULES - FROZEN
 1. Every transaction has a unique immutable internal ID.

 2. Every event-related transaction links to Event ID and Meal Session where applicable.

 3. General business transactions use an approved Overhead / Cost Centre instead of a fake Event ID.

 4. No direct stock edit, direct balance edit, silent payment edit or silent closed-event edit is allowed.

 5. Create, Submit, Verify, Approve, Reject, Pay, Reopen and Cancel are separate permission actions.

 6. All important calculations are backend-authoritative.

 7. Historical commercial and cost snapshots are preserved.

 8. Failed or duplicate submissions must be idempotent and must not create duplicate financial/stock effects.

 9. Every workflow supports attachments/evidence where the business process needs proof.

 10. Every exception requires reason, actor, timestamp and approval where configured.

 11. Unknown thresholds, limits, rates, SLAs and approval values remain configurable and are never invented in this freeze.

                           TWF #14 - EVENT CLOSING
     Prevent premature closure by reconciling stock, staff, vendor costs, customer extras, collections and
                                                 profitability.

 STATUS: FROZEN TRANSACTION WORKFLOW


 Frozen Closing Stages
 1. Operational Close - service execution complete.

 2. Return Close - stock, containers and equipment reconciled.

 3. Staff Close - attendance, OT and temporary staff settlement verified.

 4. Vendor Cost Close - outside food, purchase, rental and logistics bills verified or marked expected.

 5. Customer Close - final guest count, approved extras and billing basis confirmed.

 6. Provisional P&L; - remaining expected/missing costs visible.

 7. Financial Close - material costs/revenue complete and event locked.


 Frozen Closing Blockers
   - Unresolved stock return/variance
   - Missing critical vendor bills or expected-cost treatment
   - Unverified staff hours/settlement
   - Unbilled approved customer extras
   - Unreturned equipment / unresolved damage
   - Unresolved payment/reconciliation issue
   - Required incident/complaint action still open


 Frozen Rules
 1. Operational completion cannot directly mark Financially Closed.

 2. Late bill after close requires controlled reopen or approved post-close adjustment policy.

 3. Final Event P&L; uses actual cost where available and clearly marks provisional cost if not final.

 4. Reopening records reason, approver, old close state and new changes.

 5. Closed event source transactions remain auditable and cannot be silently edited.


 Owner Configuration Required
   - Closing SLA
   - Late-bill materiality policy
   - Who can reopen
   - Allowed post-close adjustments

   - On Hand != Reserved != Available != Incoming.
   - Invoice Value != Collected Cash != Outstanding.
   - Operational Complete != Financially Closed.

   - Sensitive approval uses source record, actor, role, amount/impact, reason, evidence and timestamp.
