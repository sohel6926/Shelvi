Frozen Rules

2. Brand is the customer-facing commercial identity.

3. Branch is the operational business location.

4. Kitchen is a production unit and is separate from Store.

5. Store is the inventory holding unit.

7. Architecture supports approved shared Branch/Kitchen use across multiple Brands.

8. Every stock balance belongs to a valid physical or logical stock location.

9. No transactional history is deleted when a master is inactivated or archived.

10. Sensitive changes are audited and may require approval.
