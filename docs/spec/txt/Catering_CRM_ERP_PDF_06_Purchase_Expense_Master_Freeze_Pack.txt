 1. Freeze Scope & Non-Negotiable Rules
 This pack consolidates the purchase master lists, folder structure, search/scroll behaviour, staff-wise purchase tracking,
 outside purchases and additional expense sections requested for the catering CRM/ERP.
 1       One Item Master record per real item. Different spellings, singular/plural forms and local names become aliases, not
         new item rows.
 2       Purchase transactions can be unlimited. Duplicate transactions are different from duplicate master items.
 3       Normal stock purchases must ultimately flow through Purchase/GRN/Stock Movement. Do not create a parallel stock
         database.
 4       Outside ready-made food/services use the existing universal Outsource engine, not a separate hard-coded CRM for
         each food.
 5       Temporary staff payments use Staff Advance/Settlement; transport uses Logistics/Expense; utilities and rent use
         Expense/Overhead; live-counter service cost links to Event/Session where applicable.
 6       Logged-in staff identity must automatically attach to purchase transactions.
 7       All important edits, cancellations, reversals and deletions are audited. Posted stock/finance history must not
         disappear.
 8       Payment status should be Finance-derived whenever a finance transaction exists.
 9       Every Event-related purchase/expense should link to Event and Meal Session where applicable; general overhead
         uses an Overhead Cost Centre.
 10      Search must support partial matching and aliases; long lists must use a sticky search bar and vertical scrolling.

     Core architecture               Easy UI is allowed. Simplified backend is not. A user may enter a purchase from one screen, but the
     safeguard                       system must still create the correct authoritative transactions underneath.

 3.1 Duplicate Prevention on Add Item
 1        Normalize case, spaces and punctuation.
 2        Search exact name, alias, singular/plural variants and common English-letter Telugu spellings.
 3        Show likely duplicate warning before creation.
 4        Allow staff to choose an existing item; only authorized Admin/Master-Data role may create a genuinely new item.
 5        Store alternate names/aliases on the same record.
 6        Never auto-merge historical transactions without approved master-data governance.
