 B5. Segregation of Duties
  Process                              Frozen Separation

  Purchase                             Requester != receiver != bill verifier != payer for sensitive/high-value flow.

  Stock adjustment                     Counter/Store submits; configured supervisor approves material variance.

  Refund                               Accounts prepares; Finance verifies; authorized approver approves; payment posted
                                       separately.

  Payroll                              Attendance verified -> HR calculates -> Finance verifies -> authorized approval -> payment.

  Customer discount                    Sales requests; configured manager/owner approves above limit.

  Event reopen                         Operations/Accounts request; authorized role approves; audit captures reason.

  Vendor master bank change            Creator/editor cannot silently cause payment destination change without controlled verification.

  Role/permission change               System Admin may configure; business owner authorizes sensitive access policy.


 OWNER CONFIGURATION REQUIRED - NOT INVENTED

   - Exact approval limits by role/module.
   - Whether branch managers exist as separate role in final org.
   - Delegation/backup approver assignments.
   - Exact export permissions.
   - Whether MFA is mandatory for all users or only privileged roles.
